# Release Note — Coach and client chat upgrade

**Date:** 5 October 2026

Chat between the Health Coach and the client is one conversation across the portal (**Messages** and the client-file chat) and the mobile app. These options were added:

---

## Day labels

Messages are grouped by **Today / Yesterday / date**.

**Why:** In a long history it was unclear which day a message belonged to, so follow-up was hard.

---

## Reply

From the three-dot menu, reply to the other person’s message or your own. A quote stays on the new message. Tapping the quote jumps to the original.

**Why:** Without this, it was unclear which message an answer belonged to, and people had to scroll to find it.

---

## Copy

From the same menu, the message text is copied.

**Why:** A link, instruction, or long text should not have to be selected by hand.

---

## Delete

Only your own message. After Confirm, both sides see “This message was deleted”.

**Why:** A sent-by-mistake message needs to be taken down. Deleting it only for yourself leaves the other person confused.

---

## React

On the **other person’s** message, with 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏. You cannot react to your own message. A reaction they put on your message is still visible.

**Why:** A short acknowledgment should not need a separate message. Reacting to your own message does not make sense.

This is separate from like / dislike on **AI Copilot** replies.

---

## Live updates

New messages, Reply, Delete, and React show up on the portal and the app almost immediately.

**Why:** If one side sends and the other does not see it, session coordination and daily follow-up break.

---

## Online / Offline

Only the **other person’s** status is shown. In the app it sits next to **Health Coach**.

The coach is online when they are active in the **Holisticare portal** — even on another page, not only inside that chat. After a few minutes of no activity they go Offline.

**Why:** The client needs to know whether the coach is available right now. Showing your own status is unnecessary.

---

## Settings → Privacy

One Privacy page instead of a separate menu for each small option.

- **Show my online status to patients** — when off, the client does not see the coach’s Online / Offline. Default is on.
- **Show Tutorial** — the previous tutorial setting, moved here.
- Later rows (read receipts, typing, notification privacy, data sharing) live on this page and are not active yet.

**Why:** Some coaches do not want patients to know they are online. The rest of privacy settings should grow in one place, not as a new menu for every switch.
